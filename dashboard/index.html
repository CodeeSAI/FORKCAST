Offline inference · India · 1° grid

What it does FORKCAST is a compact neural weather model. It projects the next 1–14 days from a real three-day input, then lets you compare that baseline with a simulated change to the starting conditions.

It explores scenarios; it does not issue weather warnings or predict damage.
